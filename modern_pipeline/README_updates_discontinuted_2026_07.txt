**UPDATES SHIFTED TO GRAPH DOWNLOADS**

Please note that a significant revision is made to the data processing pipeline in September 2026.
Consequently, live data should now be downloaded directly from the graphs (via buttons in the top-right corner of each graph).
This folder will no longer be updated.

We have made every effort to make data available in the similar format to maintain legacy compatibility, 
for any questions on the data structure please contact ben.mcwilliams@bruegel.org. 

Two explicit methodological changes: 
- Week numbering for the year 2021 has changed to align with standard ISO-formatting. Implication is that the old week 53 of year 2021
has become week 52. To compare this legacy dataset with the new data, shift all year 2021 weeks one forward (ie 53 becomes 52, 52 becomes 51). 
- We continue to use a factor of 10.3 to convert gas flows reported by ENTSOG in kWh into million cubic metres. 
For Norwegian flows, we have adjusted the conversion factor to 11 kWh/m³. This follows a comparison of our estimates with official Gassco data, 
and is consistent with the relatively high calorific value of Norwegian pipeline gas. The implication is that all Norway values are slightly
lower in the live database than reported in this file. 

Part of the update also involved refreshing our historic cache of data from providors (Bloomberg, ENTSO-G and ALSI.GIE) meaning some historic 
data are marginally adjusted.
